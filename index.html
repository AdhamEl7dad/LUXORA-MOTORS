Who We Are

About Luxora Motors

A premium automotive showroom built on a passion for engineering excellence and timeless design.

Our Story

Founded by a small team of collectors and enthusiasts, Luxora Motors began with a single belief: that an extraordinary car deserves an extraordinary stage. Today, our showroom curates the world's most coveted marques under one roof.

Every vehicle in our collection is hand-selected, inspected, and presented with the care it deserves — from studio-grade photography to a private, pressure-free viewing experience.

Our Mission & Vision

Our mission is to make the acquisition of exceptional automobiles as effortless and transparent as possible — pairing every client with the car that fits their ambition.

Our vision is to become the most trusted name in premium automotive retail, recognized for integrity, curation, and an unmatched client experience.

Curated Collection

Every vehicle is hand-selected from the world's finest marques and inspected before it reaches our floor.

Transparent Service

Clear, honest guidance at every step — from your first enquiry to the moment you drive away.

Client-First Experience

Private viewings, tailored finance conversations, and a showroom designed to feel like a gallery.

Aftercare Excellence

Concierge-level aftercare, detailing partnerships, and support long after the handover.

Project 1: Cinematic Color Grading

A bright daylight photograph of an Aston Martin DB12 graded into a cinematic look: teal-black shadows, warm golden highlights, deeper contrast, a soft highlight glow and a darkened vignette.

Project 2: Background Replacement

A black Range Rover shot in a plain white studio is moved into a dark luxury showroom with a spotlight and light panels; the white walls are replaced and the floor is darkened to match.

Project 3: Blemish & Distraction Removal

An Audi RS7 photographed against a street background: the distracting light poles above the wall are removed with content-aware inpainting, leaving the car and the clean sky untouched.

Photoshop-style techniques used (to be repeated in Adobe Photoshop):

Project 4: Dodge & Burn Enhancement

A Lamborghini Revuelto with flat lighting: highlights are dodged, shadows are burned, local contrast is increased and the details are sharpened for more depth.
